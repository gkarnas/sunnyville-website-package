Put your roof to work.
Keep more of your money.

Solar can turn sunshine into savings on your electricity bills. Over time, those savings can recover the upfront cost of your system.

01 / SAVE

Buy less electricity

Use the power your panels generate to run your home during the day, reducing how much electricity you buy from the grid.

02 / STORE

Use your solar after sunset

A battery stores spare solar energy for later, helping reduce grid use in the evening. We assess whether the extra savings justify the battery cost for your household.

03 / PAYBACK

Savings that add up

Once accumulated savings cover the system’s upfront cost, further savings can put you ahead financially, allowing for maintenance, finance and replacement costs.

Your payback period depends on the installed price, electricity tariffs, roof conditions and how much solar energy you use. We can assess your electricity bills and explain the estimated savings and payback before you decide.
